Identity pitch · Three concepts

The Smoky Barrel

A barbecue restaurant with an unusual claim: the pit runs on physics. Every brisket is smoked against a model — heat and moisture transport, the stall predicted before it happens. The identity should feel like the place: charred steel drums, butcher paper, and one quietly nerdy secret in the mark.

Construction drawing · the plume is the cook’s temperature curve

Concept A — The Stall

Selected · leads the identity

A drum smoker whose plume rises, drifts flat, and rises again — the exact shape of a brisket’s internal-temperature curve through the stall. To most people it’s just good-looking smoke. To anyone who has watched a brisket sit at 165°F for four hours, it’s an inside joke. No other barbecue joint can claim this mark; it’s drawn from your own model’s output.

Concept B — Cooper’s Mark

Selected · badge, stamps & merch

The barrel seen head-on: two hoops, riveted, with the house smoke — fat puffs tracing the stall — and one live coal at its root. Built like a cooper’s stamp — it wants to be burned into wood, inked onto butcher paper, and embroidered on caps.

text ring drops below 48px; keep hoops + smoke only

Where it works: wax seals on to-go boxes, coasters, the branded butcher-paper roll, shirt backs. Pairs with Concept A rather than competing with it.

Concept C — Smoke Ring

Not advancing · kept for reference

The cross-section every pitmaster is judged by: dark bark, the pink smoke ring, the tender center — abstracted into a half-round rising over the cutting board. It reads as a sunrise until you’ve seen a brisket sliced, and then it never reads as anything else.

Julia Edition

Concept A selected · exported to assets/logo.svg

Every mark carries all four Julia brand colors for a registered BrisketDynamics.jl (or SmokyBarrel.jl) — split so no one color outweighs the rest. The drum’s three panels are the three dots stacked — red, green, purple — with a red coal glowing in the firebox, under smoke in wordmark blue (pitmasters chase “thin blue smoke,” so the physics checks out). The badge quarters its outer hoop into all four colors, evenly. The slice was born balanced: purple bark, red ring, green center, blue board. Shown on README white and Documenter dark.

Naming

Recommended
The Smoky Barrel

“The” makes it a place — you go to the Smoky Barrel. Singular matches every mark on this page: one drum, one barrel head, one slice. Strongest on signage and easiest to say out loud.

Keep in reserve
Smoky Barrels

Plural reads like a product line or a multi-location group. Worth holding as the parent name if a second pit ever opens — the marks scale to it by dropping “The” from the lockup.
