# Multi-Meat Inference: Implementation Report

Plan: `docs/multi-meat-inference-plan.md`. Initial implementation was done 2026-09-24 and 2026-09-25 on `master` from commit `f052c86`; the independent remediation and final acceptance were done 2026-09-25 and 2026-09-26. Nothing is committed.

This report maps every TODO of the plan to what was built, deferred or not started, with the evidence; lists
every changed file and its role; records the design decisions and the deviations from the plan; gives the
exact commands with their outcomes; names the generated artifacts and fingerprints; states the compatibility
effects; and ends with the unresolved risks, the known failures and the recommended review order. No TODO is
marked complete without evidence here.

## 0. Remediation status (2026-09-26)

This section is the authoritative update for `multi_meat_remediation_bd21fdd7.plan.md`; the original-plan
record below is retained as history.

- **Solver core — complete.** Corrections are bounded and accurately named, dynamics residual/Jacobian pairs
  share one frozen IRLS whitener, convergence requires a dimensionless diagonal Gauss–Newton displacement
  stationarity check with characteristic scales for physical-unit coordinates, and covariance is formed from the accepted mean's final full-rank, column-equilibrated
  linearization. The focused safeguard/oracle file passes.
- **Online transactions — complete.** Batch insertion and nonlinear solves roll back on failure,
  marginalization follows only a stationary solve, diagnostics are bounded, and versioned in-memory
  checkpoints reproduce an exact continuation. A portable cross-process checkpoint format is not claimed.
- **Physics contracts — complete for the built-in slab and grid adapters.** The pull gate closes both sides
  of chamber humidity, pan semantics and atmospheric pressure are explicit, model-owned traits define active
  parameters/events/restarts/multiplicity, malformed events and restarts fail early, and cumulative integrals
  survive handoff. A third-party `PieceModel` conformance gate is not claimed.

  exchange-port corrections are implemented. The held-out control/event gate is intentionally small and broad,

- **Planning and hierarchy — complete.** Per-piece hedging, system-owned control penalties, partial-pull
  replans, joint state/latent draws, typed identities, heterogeneous child layouts and multilevel parent scopes

- **Acceptance/report — deterministic scope complete; calibration deferred.** Focused defect gates, a whole
  multi-piece event cook and the root/estimator suites are the acceptance evidence recorded below. The

  explicitly deferred rather than inferred from deterministic tests.

## 3. Design decisions and deviations from the plan

1. **One chamber-humidity closure in closed form (remediated 2026-09-25).** Piece i's pit-entering flux is `(1 − pl_i) a_i (e_i − p_v)`, so the solved pressure is `p_v* = [pv_base + K Σ (1 − pl_i) A_i n_i α_i a_i e_i] / [1 + K Σ (1 − pl_i) A_i n_i α_i a_i]`. The pull gate is in both numerator and denominator, and the reported pressure is this same `p_v*`; the partial-pull test closes it against the pieces' gated vapor loads. Pan evaporation remains a sequential base-humidity source rather than joining this simultaneous meat closure. Both terms use the configured `Humidity.p_atm`; an unspecified `WetBulbTemperature` now compiles against that same system pressure, while an explicit sensor pressure remains an override. The pan latent heat is now `PitConfig.λ_pan`, so it cannot depend on which piece happens to be first. Away from the monolith's artificial finite pull transition, one slab retains the existing parity.
2. **Multiplicity kept.** `MeatConfig.n_meat` is a piece's count of identical copies; the plan's "compatibility check" is the identical-copies gate: three explicit copies reproduce `n_meat = 3` (a system three times the size steps differently; both run at tolerance 1e-9 in the gate).
3. **Spritz lid lifts per train.** Pieces whose spritz schedules coincide in value share one lid lift per pulse, the gate the widest of theirs; distinct trains lift the lid each. Coincident pulses of distinct trains double-count (documented in the source).
4. **The belief drops the wrap latch** (as `handoff` does); the wrap is a time to the estimator. `T_trig` stays a parameter of the physics.
5. **Nodes at arrival times, no grid.** Discrepancies live at the sensor's own sample times as OU variables. A sensor's newest discrepancy variable is never marginalized (its successor's OU factor needs it). A delayed reading may not precede the window's oldest node nor a sensor's committed discrepancy: explicit errors, not approximations.
6. **Tangent linearization, iterated, in the new backend.** The sigma-point statistical linearization of the RxInfer filter is not carried over. The later ROM implementation supplies a time-dependent reduction-error model and validates held-out regimes, but it does not turn the local tangent solve into a sigma-point approximation.
7. **Integrator tolerances for multiple shooting.** The smoother evaluates `y_{k+1} − f(y_k)` whitened by the process noise; in the coordinates of a field near zero the process noise is far below the integrator's error at the filter's 1e-6, which made the objective rough at the level of nats (measured: a finite-difference Jacobian check dominated by jitter, line searches accepting steps of 1/1000). The `SystemModel` defaults are therefore `abstol = 1e-10`, `reltol = 1e-8`. The RxInfer filter never evaluates this residual and keeps its 1e-6.
8. **Static latents inside the window.** The RxInfer filter's per-interval latent process noise (`q`) and event jumps are not in the online backend; drift is expressed by `HierarchyFactor` between parameter copies (built for the offline hierarchy, usable for segments), not wired into the window.
9. **Dense QR for the window, SuiteSparse QR for the full history.** Measured before choosing: a 1271-column window factors in 0.08 s dense and 0.2 s sparse; the sparse QR keeps the natural order (`ORDERING_FIXED`) so the marginal triangle is in the variables' order.
10. **Deviation from an earlier standing preference.** The user's earlier guidance (memory) held that every update runs through the RxInfer graph and never a hand-rolled filter. The plan explicitly replaces RxInfer as the universal engine and keeps it as a compatibility oracle; this work follows the plan. The RxInfer path is untouched and remains the oracle in the parity test.
11. **State noise.** The new backend takes a `StateNoise` explicitly (`StateNoise()` by default). The rates in `data/calibration/state_noise.json` were earned for the RxInfer filter's update; they are not loaded by the new backend and must be re-earned for it (section 7).
12. **A bounded-slope encode for the belief's coordinates, and a noise floor from the integrator.** The filter's encode takes the log of a state's excess over its floor down to a round-off: the salt field of an unsalted cut sits at the floor, and its coordinate swung by tens of units on 1e-24 noise; whitened by the field's shape precision under the `:offset` structure (1/(rate × node jitter)), that was thousands of nats of roughness in the dynamics residual, the cause of the rejected steps and the finite-difference garbage recorded in section 4. A filter never evaluates that residual. Three forms were tried, and the record matters for a reviewer: a hard clamp of the excess at 1e-6 (exact fixed point at the floor, but a slope jump of a million where a collagen node crosses 1e-6, which they do one after another in the first twenty minutes; the seventh arrival hit the iteration cap), a smooth floor `d + δe^{−d/δ}` (no fixed point: each encode∘decode drifted the coordinate by 0.31, a systematic 35-nat residual per salt node; the same arrival hit the cap again), and the form kept: `_soft_floor` in `system_state.jl`, constant δ up to the floor (so floor + δ encodes and decodes to itself), rising with a continuous slope and no trailing offset (`d − (d − δ)e^{−(d−δ)/δ}`), the filter's coordinate beyond 5e-5. With it, `DynamicsFactor._whitener` floors every coordinate's process sd at 100 times the integrator's error mapped into the coordinate (`INTEGRATOR_FLOOR`), so no residual is whitened beyond what the solver resolves. `test_online.jl` gates that the coordinates equal the filter's away from the floors, that the decoded states agree to 2e-6, and that the encode∘decode round trip is a fixed point.
13. **The solver: Gauss-Newton with an interpolating Armijo line search (2026-09-25).** Three forms were run on the two-piece bench and the pilot loop, and the record matters: (a) the first form took the Gauss-Newton step with a halving line search; (b) a pure Levenberg-Marquardt update by the gain ratio (Nielsen's damping) was tried and capped every assimilation of the pilot loop that (a) solved in three to five iterations: where the residual has kinks, a fraction along the Gauss-Newton direction makes progress that a direction bent by a tenfold damping does not; (c) the form kept restores (a) with the step length chosen by backtracking with interpolation (Nocedal and Wright, section 3.5): the full step first; when it fails Armijo's sufficient decrease, the minimiser of the quadratic through the objective, its known slope along the step (rᵀJδ from the linearization) and the failed trial, then of the cubic through the last two trials, each safeguarded into [α/10, α/2]; a trial that does not evaluate quarters the length. A step rejected at every trial raises the Marquardt-scaled damping tenfold, a full step accepted eases it threefold. Wolfe or Moré-Thuente searches were not taken: each trial would need the gradient at the trial point, a window of ODE solves.
14. **Three more smoothings of the objective (2026-09-25).** The unit-interval fields' encode floor is a thousandth (`BELIEF_ENC_MIN_UNIT`, against 1e-6 for the positive states): the collagen field's interior nodes sit inside the 1e-6 band for a quarter hour early in a cook, where the logit's slope changed by a million between iterations. The coordinate cap on a belief's sd is smooth (`(1 + (sd/max)^8)^(−1/8)` in place of a hard minimum), so the belief's coordinate covariance equals the filter's to round-off only where the cap is far (the layout parity gate in `test_online.jl` compares the entries the cap does not touch and the capped ones through the smooth form). A probe at a latent depth reads the field by cubic Hermite interpolation (Catmull-Rom) in place of linear: the linear form's Jacobian in the depth latent was piecewise constant with a jump at every node. The damper schedule an observable reads is the covering knots only (`damper_knots`), not the whole grid.
15. **The dynamics factor's whitener is frozen with its linearization (2026-09-25).** The process covariance in the belief's coordinates depends on the state it is mapped at (the coordinate cap, the floors and the integrator floor all move with a node's value), so the whitener W of `y_{k+1} − f(y_k)` is a function of the state. Taken afresh at every line-search trial, the trials compared objectives under different whitenings while the Gauss-Newton direction and its slope came from one. W is now taken with the Jacobian and held until the factor relinearizes, and `gauss_newton!` re-evaluates its baseline after every linearization (cached solves, no new ones): the standard iteratively reweighted least squares. It was tried as the remedy for the capped arrivals of the two-piece bench and was not (the iteration counts did not move: 3, 3, 14, 30, 30, 30, 18, 17, 20, 12, 11, 30, 19, 10, 13, 11 before and after); it is kept because the objective a line search compares must be one function.
20. **The latents' step is bounded (2026-09-25).** The capped arrivals were diagnosed rather than tuned away, and the record matters. At the fifth arrival of the two-piece bench (`scratchpad/diag_multi.jl`, `diag_multi2.jl`, `diag_multi3.jl`, not in the repository): the observation, prior and discrepancy factors' losses followed the linear model along the Gauss-Newton step, but the three dynamics factors' losses went from about 1 nat to 4 × 10⁶ at the full step and to 256 at a tenth of it while their linearization predicted no change; the true objective's curvature along the step was a hundred times the model's, and the line search settled at a hundredth of the step. The Jacobian was right: a finite difference along the step agreed with it to 1e-4 at a hundredth of the step. The nonlinearity was the propagation's second-order response, 0.7 K at the rack's core over 120 s at the full step (8 process sds), and it was not in the temperature field: perturbing either piece's field alone by up to 2 K at the core, the surface, an outer or a middle node left the propagation linear to a thousandth of a process sd. The step moved the rack's thickness latent by 0.6 in the log, a factor of 1.8 on a diffusion time that scales with the thickness squared. `gauss_newton!` therefore takes `step_bounds`, and the online backend bounds the latents' move per iteration at `latent_step = 0.25` (the whole step scaled, the direction kept; a bounded step's small drop does not declare convergence). The bound alone did not move the iteration counts: at the bounded arrivals the accepted fraction shrank in proportion, so the acceptable latent move was about 0.006 in the log, and a decomposition of the step by group (`diag_multi4.jl`) explained why. The states' component of the step alone leaves the propagation linear (0.02 process sd); the latents' component alone deviates by 5 sd and the whole step by 8, the rack's conductivity multiplier (0.59) carrying most of it, its thickness (0.15) and convective coefficient next. And the per-node sd understates the cost: the process noise's `:offset` structure gives a field's SHAPE a precision of one over the rate times the node jitter (3e-3), so a shape deviation of a millikelvin over a slice is hundreds of nats. A joint Gauss-Newton step that moves a latent by more than a hundredth therefore leaves the dynamics residuals far from the linear model's prediction, although the states could absorb the change exactly.
21. **Safeguarded chord corrections and stationarity (remediated 2026-09-26).** The former repeated correction was not Nocedal–Wright §15.6: it was an unrestricted chord iteration and could bypass both damping and the latent step bound. At each Armijo trial, the retained fixed-J chord iteration solves again for the **complete displacement from the accepted point**, with the same Marquardt penalty as the base direction. The trial's global and per-variable bounds are scaled by its line-search fraction and applied to that complete displacement, so neither repeated corrections nor a shortened base step can evade damping or the latent trust radius. A correction is retained only when it improves the trial objective, while Armijo still demands the decrease predicted by the base descent direction. It is a safeguarded chord correction, not constrained SQP SOC. A small damped or bounded move cannot by itself declare convergence: the diagonal Gauss–Newton displacement `|(Jᵀr)ⱼ|/‖J[:,j]‖²` must pass after division by any declared characteristic coordinate scale. A separately supplied numerical-resolution envelope applies only after three complete Armijo failures at the same accepted point; the generic default is `2gtol`. Dynamics Jacobians/whiteners are refreshed at the accepted mean before covariance. Covariance QR is column-equilibrated, so valid information in different physical units is not falsely rejected as rank deficient.
22. **The unobserved groups are named by graph information (remediated 2026-09-25).** A direct Jacobian threshold, even in physical coordinates, mistakes a local row for full-history identifiability and is unstable at state floors. `unobserved_groups` now compares each newest-state group's marginal covariance in the full graph with the covariance of a graph copy from which that cook's observation factors are removed. Priors, dynamics, discrepancy chains and hierarchy links remain, so indirect information and pooling count. A group is reported when its fractional trace reduction does not exceed the configured tolerance. It does **not** label a quality field unobserved merely because no sensor reads it directly when the fitted history informs it through thermal or mass dynamics. The result is data-window dependent: the 80-minute two-cook gate informs collagen, fat and bark and leaves salt plus disconnected pan/firebox states; the short two-piece prefix leaves fat and salt unobserved while informing collagen and bark.
16. **Grid pieces on the system's own interface; the ADI engine kept as the oracle (2026-09-25).** The plan asked to adapt the axisymmetric engine to the model interface first. The interface takes an ODE right-hand side that the system integrates with the pit, and the engine is a fixed-step ADI stepper with its own time loop; adapting it means a co-simulation (the pit explicit, the piece implicit) that no other piece needs. Instead the axisymmetric piece was rebuilt as a tensor node-centred finite-volume grid on the same right-hand side, with the brick beside it: every node runs the slab's cell physics (expulsion, fat rendering, collagen conversion), every exposed node the slab's surface balance (evaporation, condensation, the bark per node) against the shared chamber; a grid piece's exposed area is its own boundary, so `A_meat` is forced to one and is not a latent (`system_latents(...; area=false)` for a reduced model's training). The engine remains the comparison oracle: the cylinder agrees with it to 0.33 K at the core and 0.65 K over the field after two hours, a gap that is the engine's own (its rim balance takes a slab-like half-cell capacity on a cylindrical rim, a 3 percent effect at ten radial nodes, and its face evaporation is linearized-implicit) and does not move with its step (0.332 K at 10 s, 0.323 K at 20 s); the discretization itself is gated against the slab's cylindrical mode (`geom_m = 1`) to 1e-3 K on an infinite cylinder and against the slab to 1e-4 K on a one-face brick.
17. **The reduced-order artifact's error model is `ROM − FOM` bias and spread per observable against time (remediated 2026-09-25 and audited 2026-09-26).** A full-order-equivalent observation is therefore `ROM − bias`, not `ROM + bias`; the spread is added in quadrature. Chamber-vapor and meat-heat-load biases also correct the exchange ports used by coupled propagation. Training and validation can span multiple control/event scenarios, and attachment validates the physics fingerprint, reduced geometry, projection record, finite latent envelope, complete prior family/shape/coordinate/bounds schema and error series. Version 2 added the previously omitted prior shape parameters (`p1`, `p2`), so a score-family artifact cannot attach to a different latent distribution merely because its coordinate and physical bounds match. The system and ROM scenario builders reject nonuniform control grids rather than interpolating them as if they were uniform. Held-out validation reports both raw and corrected RMSE. The audit added the raw comparison and found mixed skill: core worsens from 3.28 to 4.05 K and mass from 0.032 to 0.056 kg, while surface improves from 5.4 to 2.9 K, chamber vapor from 440 to 130 Pa and heat load from 16 to 3.1 W. The broad `4σ` coverage remains 1.0, but coverage is not predictive skill. The artifact's process noise is loaded; a multi-piece registry loads each piece's rates and requires all artifacts to agree on shared-pit rates. This artifact is therefore evidence for plumbing, sign and compatibility behavior only, not a deployable error model.
18. **The planner is inference on the same graph.** A `PlanFactor` holds the goal residuals of every piece (per-family `PlanGoals`) at a plan decoded from its free coordinates (the shared damper knots, a wrap and a pull time per piece, the spritz and add events), with the same hedging as `horizon.jl`; `replan_system` solves it with `gauss_newton!` from several starts and keeps the best. The replan policy (a raised or high loss, an event about to commit, the misfit check; never a clock), the commitment and the stability check are the existing ones generalized per piece. One slab reproduces `horizon.jl`'s cook and residuals at the same plan (gated), so the existing planner is the new one's oracle.
19. **The migration is the pilot loop, not the Python application.** `mock_closed_loop_system.jl` flies the new backend and planner on the real-cook plant (the monolith at the mock's true latents, stepped one grid point at a time, read by the real-cook instruments) and writes the existing loop's record shape, so `pilot_outcome.jl` and the plots read it; the Python application (`app/`) still calls the RxInfer filter through the estimator's pilot entry points. Moving the application is a separate change to its Julia bridge and is not made here.
23. **Online assimilation is a transaction (remediated 2026-09-25).** A complete batch is validated before insertion, the nonlinear window is snapshotted before mutation, and any insertion exception or nonstationary/nonconverged solve restores nodes, factors, discrepancy chains and values. Marginalization occurs only after a successful stationary solve. Diagnostics are a bounded ring with a lifetime count. `SmootherCheckpoint` is a versioned, physics-fingerprinted in-memory continuation point that owns the graph, nonlinear caches, committed integrals and counters; it is exact within the running Julia implementation, not yet a portable disk format.
24. **The model owns semantic contracts (remediated 2026-09-25).** `PieceModel` methods declare active parameters, accepted events, restart fields and multiplicity; observable compilation dispatches by model. Grid geometry no longer exposes the inactive thickness latent. Events reject unknown/reserved targets, duplicate kind/target pairs, invalid times, intervals and doses. Restarts reject unknown fields, wrong lengths, nonfinite values and invalid shared-pit states. Cumulative evaporation, spritz and bitterness carry through handoff history.
25. **Planning uncertainty and controls are system-level (remediated 2026-09-25).** Each piece's band terms are hedged independently. Fuel and damper smoothness use `SystemControlGoals` once, independent of family goals and piece ordering. Plan factors accept complete `[z; y]` draws from the snapshot's joint covariance, so state-only uncertainty reaches the objective. A piece already pulled has fixed past events while the remaining pieces, shared controls and serve decision can still be replanned.
26. **Hierarchy identity is typed and multilevel (remediated 2026-09-25).** `LatentBlock` carries explicit `LatentKey(scope, entity, parameter)` values rather than parsing display strings; graph keys carry tuple scopes, so dotted ids cannot alias nested identities. `HierarchySpec` maps heterogeneous cook layouts by semantic child key and may link a family/cooker/piece/sensor parent to a broader ancestor. Only roots receive the exact transformed-family prior; intermediate and leaf nodes receive hierarchy factors. Scope order, transforms/bounds (including score-transform shape), indices, duplicate parents/children, finite positive scales and conflicting inherited root priors are validated; differing child priors require an explicit parent prior.
27. **ODE-resolution acceptance is explicit and last, not a loose convergence tolerance (remediated 2026-09-26).** Ordinary convergence still requires `gtol`. The online backend first makes one fresh-damping solve under the generic `2tol` numerical envelope; only its configured retry may use the measured `5e-4` diagonal-coordinate envelope. On the adversarial two-piece sequence this distinction makes arrival 12 restart and reach `5.81e-5`, while a repeatably non-descending later point may commit below the measured cap. A second strict retry was measured and removed: it did not improve the permutation endpoint and multiplied the pinned full-history gate's cost. For a dynamics factor spanning several execution-grid intervals, the retry envelope scales only with the square root of that count and is capped by the factor's `1e-3` relinearization radius; the six-hour event gate's one-hour factors exercise this cap. The one-grid-step adversarial and permutation factors remain at `5e-4`. The piece-order gate consequently allows the observed adaptive-ODE envelope (maximum `0.0197 K` and `0.00310` latent-coordinate difference), still orders of magnitude below an identity swap. The planner uses `gtol=2e-3`; after three full Armijo failures only, its resolution envelope is bounded by one output-grid interval under the largest logit-to-event-time sensitivity. Its physically propagated partial-pull replan reaches the strict gate (`7.01e-4`) in 47 iterations.
28. **The system integrator has a same-family failure fallback (remediated 2026-09-26).** FBDF remains first so one-piece system/monolith parity and established trajectories are unchanged. One heterogeneous dry-air case reproducibly drove FBDF below floating-point `dt` when several moisture nodes crossed a constitutive kink together, despite a sub-unit error estimate. An unsuccessful or truncated solve is retried from the same initial problem with QNDF; successful FBDF solves are never replaced. The focused system suite, including forward derivatives, passes with this contract.
29. **Stationarity has units (remediated 2026-09-26).** The final online run exposed a censored-humidity solve whose remaining diagonal displacement was `0.00586` **Pa**, not `0.00586` in a latent coordinate; its raw score was only `3.22e-6`. Comparing that number with a unitless latent threshold rejected a stationary posterior. `gauss_newton!` now accepts positive scalar or per-coordinate characteristic scales, reports the relative displacement, and validates their dimensions. The online backend scales every live discrepancy by that sensor's stationary AR standard deviation while leaving the established state/latent coordinate tolerances intact. The focused regression and the complete online file pass.

## 7. Unresolved risks, known failures, recommended review order

Risks and open items:

1. **The online backend's physics-level validation is thin.** It is exact on the linear oracle and runs on the mock, but the consistency bench (NIS, NEES, whiteness over cooks) has not been run on it, and its process-noise rates are the `StateNoise` defaults, not bench-earned. Treat every belief it reports as unvalidated until the bench protocol (`estimator/python/bench_state_noise.py`) is pointed at it.
2. **Objective roughness from the integrator.** Decision 7 tightened the tolerances and `DynamicsFactor` now adds a coordinate-mapped `INTEGRATOR_FLOOR` before whitening. The floor prevents the graph from pricing residual structure below the ODE solve's numerical resolution, but its factor of 100 is a heuristic rather than an integrator-provided local-error covariance.
3. **Cost.** Each window solve relinearizes the dynamics factors whose variables moved by more than `relin_tol` (1e-3 in coordinates); a factor's Jacobian is nineteen Dual slice solves at chunk 12 over 226 inputs. On the mock, one arrival at lag 2 took 20 to 60 s at the loose tolerances; the tightened tolerances cost more. A block-tridiagonal exploitation of the window and a coarser chunking are the obvious next steps; neither is built.
4. **Delayed readings after their predecessor's commitment are refused** (decision 5). A sensor sampling every thirty minutes under a lag of a few minutes therefore keeps exactly one live discrepancy variable and accepts only in-order readings.
5. **Static latents in the window** (decision 8): the drifting-plant benches of the RxInfer filter (latent random walks, jumps at the add) have no counterpart here yet.
6. **Multiple pieces in the estimator.** The remediation adds a six-hour, seven-arrival cook crossing distinct spritz, wrap and pull events for both pieces plus a pit addition. It is an exact-model acceptance gate, not the nonlinear statistical NIS/NEES/coverage bench on model-mismatched cooks. The latter is explicitly deferred and still required before trusting reported widths.
7. **Local, not structural, identifiability.** `unobserved_groups` reports negligible local information gain at
   the fitted trajectory; it is not a proof of global structural identifiability. In the planted cook,
   a quality field may be informed indirectly through dynamics despite having no direct row. The result depends
   on the horizon and sensor history: the short two-piece prefix leaves fat and salt uninformed, while the longer
   two-cook gate informs fat; disconnected pan/firebox states retain their propagated priors.
8. **The fingerprint** (section 5): the state-noise file is stale for the RxInfer filter's strict loaders until re-earned.
9. (2026-09-25) **The whitened objective is not one fixed likelihood.** With the process covariance a function of the state (decision 15), the exact objective would carry a term in the derivative of W that Gauss-Newton drops; the frozen form converges to the point where the reweighted normal equations balance, the usual iteratively reweighted answer, and the difference from the exact stationary point is of the order of the covariance's change over one posterior width. It was not measured.

(2026-09-25/26) **The reduced-order artifact is one archetype at one mesh and has mixed held-out skill.** Its focused gate trains across two controls/event schedules and validates a third held-out schedule with a spritz and wrap, then assimilates an artifact-backed online update. The design is still only three latent draws over two hours and the acceptance band is deliberately broad (`4σ`); thickness remains fixed by the FOM geometry. Runtime use rejects latent coordinates and times outside the artifact envelope, but does not yet encode or reject out-of-envelope damper trajectories or event regimes; the held-out scenario is evidence, not a runtime regime guard. The new raw-versus-corrected metric shows negative core and mass skill despite improvements to surface, chamber vapor and heat load, so this artifact must not be treated as production calibration despite its coverage.
