## 3. Design decisions

1. **No Python in the estimator's path.** The plan's Python-application migration was removed (no source to migrate from); the plan's "backend selector in `bench_state_noise.py`" was replaced by the Julia driver. The plot scripts render; the manifests they read are written by the Julia driver in the same schema, so the frozen `bench_manifest.py` validation accepts them.
2. **The NIS at the predicted belief.** `_predicted_consistency` removes the arrival's observation factors, takes the joint covariance of the variables they touch, restores them in their order, and forms `S = H C Hᵀ + I` with `H = ∂h/∂x / σ` (the negative of the whitened residual's Jacobian) and `ν = (y − h)/σ` at the predicted point. The dynamics Jacobian it takes is the one the solve's first iteration uses (the factor caches by point). A censored reading is not a residual and is excluded, as in the filter.
3. **The accumulator's frame and gain.** The frame is `[latents; newest node; each discrepancy sensor's newest live discrepancy]`, a sensor without a live one at its stationary prior. The transition into the frame: identity on the latents (the moving copies walk: identity too), the newest dynamics factor's Jacobian at the predicted point on the node, a sampled sensor's OU coefficient on its discrepancy (a first sample a fresh draw: zero), an idle sensor's newest standing (one). The gain is the linear gain at the predicted point `P⁻ Hᵀ S⁻¹`, as the RxInfer filter's feed uses its first linearization's; the iterated solve's effective gain differs, the same approximation as the oracle's. A failed (rolled-back) arrival is not fed: the next arrival's transition spans the gap.
4. **Sensor noise as groups.** The OU discrepancy is a state whose innovation variance is `θ (1 − ρ²)`: a process group on its coordinate (`dQ = (1−ρ²) e eᵀ` when the sensor sampled). The white variance is a measurement group: `dR = 1/(σ² S_ii)` on the sensor's standardized row (`σ` the row's whitening, the instrument's floor or the floor with a reduction spread). The first discrepancy's stationary prior and the state's initial widths are transients, not groups.
5. **The rates' sensitivity by ForwardDiff.** `_process_cov_sens` differentiates the coordinate process covariance (the physical covariance in the variance form, the coordinate map with the cap in the variance, the integrator floor) with respect to each group's variance rate. The events' disturbances and the floor do not move with a rate. The cap's variance form makes the derivative finite at a zero rate, where the square-root form was not.
6. **Relinearization tolerances split.** `DynamicsFactor` judges the latents' move and the node's move separately (`relin_tol`, `relin_tol_state`), both defaulting to the previous 1e-3, so nothing changes unless a caller asks. The exact refresh before the certificate and the covariance (decision 21 of the multi-meat report) is untouched: the profile shows it is the cost floor, and the certificate's exactness is why it stays.
7. **Latent drift as per-node copies.** The moving latents (the walking sites and the jumping sites) are one variable per window node, tied to the previous node's copy by a `HierarchyFactor` with sd `sqrt(q Δh + Σ jumps crossed)`; a slice's dynamics factor reads the copy at its start node, an observation the copy at its node; a node's copy leaves the window with it. A jump-only site has no walk between jumps and a factor cannot tie two variables exactly, so its copies are tied at `DRIFT_Q_FLOOR = 1e-6` per hour (1e-3 per √h in the latent's coordinate, 5e-3 over a day): numerically static, stated. The static block is one variable, as before; with no drift the graph is byte-for-byte the previous one. Drift and a hierarchy on one cook are refused (not built together).
8. **Matched noise is the bench's default.** The worker's sensors carry `RealCookNoise`'s OU sd and correlation time with the pilot loop's white floors (0.1 K, 0.1 K, 10 Pa, 5 g); `noise prior` reproduces the RxInfer bench's calibration-prior configuration for the comparison. A calibration file's sensor entries override either.
9. **The calibration file is the backend's own**, fingerprinted by `backend_fingerprint` (the physics and the backend's files), so the RxInfer filter's `state_noise.json` and its fingerprint are untouched. The record carries the sensors' noise beside the state noise.
10. **The graph's own consistency reads, beside NIS and NEES** (built during the rounds in a second worktree, `BrisketDynamics_dev`, since the running rounds read this tree's files; the estimator files come back here when the rounds end). NIS tests the newest node's predictive distribution and NEES the Laplace belief's spread against a truth; neither uses what the graph uniquely holds. At the accepted point every factor's whitened residual is in hand without a new solve, so `assimilate!` now records, on a committed arrival, `resid`: the newest slice's dynamics residual per state group as its sum of squares and count (unit power per coordinate when the process noise is right, less by the group's leverage; a direct, truth-free read of the rates that transfers to a real pit), every window slice's pooled, this arrival's observation and OU residual squares per sensor, and the graph's rows and columns (with the objective, the least-squares goodness of fit `2F/(rows − cols)`); and `past`: when a node leaves the window, its belief as it leaves, the smoothed past at the lag, which the worker scores against the plant at that node's time (the smoothed past is what the plan's integrals come from and was not scored before). The worker records both, the driver pools and prints them; `test_bench_system.jl` gates the record's structure at lag 1 and lag 0.

11. **The ignited fraction has its own coordinate floor** (`belief_layout` gives the pit's `ignited` the kind `:ignited`; its encode floors at `BELIEF_ENC_MIN` = 1e-6 instead of the unit kind's 1e-3; decode and its Jacobian are the unit kind's). The plant's fire saturates at exactly one within an hour or two of most cooks; with the thousandth's floor the belief's fire sat at 0.999 and the model kept spreading it at `k_spread × 10⁻³ × airflow`, a dynamics the plant does not have. Section 4c has the measurement. The collagen field keeps the thousandth (its conversion under a thousandth is nothing, and the two-piece bench's iteration cap of 2026-09-25 was its interior nodes inside a 1e-6 band).
12. **The solver reports the joint step, and the smoother's last attempt may stop on it.** `gauss_newton!` returns, beside the diagonal certificate, the undamped Gauss-Newton step at the accepted point in scaled coordinates and in the point's own posterior standard deviations (`_newton_step_metrics`, one pivoted QR of the final Jacobian; NaN for a sparse solve); the arrival record carries them as `newton` and `newton_sd`. The normal certificate is unchanged. A new keyword `newton_tol` (off by default) extends only the existing resolution exception: a solve that has failed three complete line searches at one point is accepted when, at the exact relinearization, its joint step is under `newton_tol` posterior sd — the diagonal read divides each coordinate's score by its own column only and sat at 5e-4 against 1e-4 on such a point while the joint step was 1.2e-4 sd and the objective flat to six digits (section 4c, item 5: the 72-minute arrival, 36 iterations in two attempts, damping to 1e8, rolled back). `solve_window!` passes `ONLINE_NEWTON_TOL` = 1e-2 on its final attempt only, as it does the measured resolution envelope. `test_factors.jl` gates the rule (a unit-Jacobian probe at 6e-4: refused by default and at 1e-4, accepted at 1e-2 with the read recorded).
14. **A slab field's process noise can carry smooth spatial modes beside the bulk and the jitter — and ships without them** (`FIELD_MODES` = 0 after the two-cook finding of section 4c, item 9; the machinery built for 2, `field_mode_basis`, `_group_structure` in `system_state.jl`; the layout carries each piece's mode count, a 3-D grid piece keeps the bulk alone). Under `:offset` a field's nodes shared one draw plus a per-node jitter of 0.003 of the group's rate, so any difference between the model's field SHAPE and a plant's was whitened at three thousandths of the rate: the phantom fire (section 4c, item 2) drew 0.23 per slice of `k_spread` information through the slab's surface nodes, the collagen floor band read 30 to 50 at the truth (item 1), and on a real cut the 1-D shape the meat does not have would inform every latent the same way. The modes are the polynomials in the node's depth coordinate, orthogonalized over the nodes and scaled to the bulk's norm, so a smooth shape error costs what a bulk error of the same RMS costs, while bending one node still costs 1/jitter² more (the phantom depth information of independent per-node noise, the filter's finding, stays excluded). The whitener and the accumulator's rate sensitivity share the structure; a field's NEES is scored on the mode coefficients (three degrees of freedom); the record and the calibration file carry `field_modes`; the frozen filter's `StateNoise` and `state_noise_cov` are untouched, and `test_online.jl`'s parity check now states the difference as exactly the modes' part. The bench's ALS identifies one rate per group as before; the split between bulk and modes is structural. With the count at zero every number is the previous structure's; the modes drifted the probe's depth latent to 6.5 sd in one cook (item 9) and wait for the mismatched-plant bench.
13. **NEES and NIS are the acceptance metrics; the residual reads are diagnostics.** Stated after the rounds' questions: the belief handed to the planner is the Gaussian, so NEES tests exactly the claim that is shipped, and a site whose posterior is not Gaussian fails it truthfully (the remedy is representational, per site, not a different metric). The residual power and the smoothed past (decision 10) locate a failure; they do not replace the acceptance.
